It is not an official view of the Federal Reserve, the Bank of Japan, the European Central Bank, the Bank of Canada, the Reserve Bank of New Zealand, the Reserve Bank of Australia, the Swiss National Bank or the Bank of England. It is Quant Flow Capital's own work and is not a copy of any other firm's research. The chances shown are our judgement and can be wrong.

How this page is updated: every day, a program collects the latest official numbers (from each central bank, statistics office and treasury, and from FRED, which republishes U.S. data) and reads new central-bank statements, minutes and speeches. An AI model then rewrites the explanations, report cards and chances following Quant Flow Capital's written rules. Automatic checks block invented quotations, impossible chances and links to unofficial sites. The text is written by an AI model and published without review by a person. Numbers that are not yet refreshed automatically are marked as hand-checked snapshots.
