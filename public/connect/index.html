Frequently Asked Questions

How We Collaborate With Institutional Capital

Everything you need to know about our research methodology, data distribution, and engagement formats.

What makes Quant Flow Capital's macro research distinct?

We do not rely on lagging anecdotal narratives. Every view is anchored by automated empirical indicators (yield curves, breakevens, money supply, central bank balance sheets) combined with first-principles geopolitical risk tracking.

How frequently is the Central Bank Monitor updated?

The monitor is refreshed daily via automated data pipelines directly connecting to official central banks, national statistical agencies, and bond yield series across 8 major currency jurisdictions.

Can institutional clients request custom research or scenario models?

Yes. We frequently develop bespoke sovereign risk briefs, interest-rate sensitivity stress tests, and geopolitical supply shock scenarios tailored to specific portfolio positioning mandates.

Is Quant Flow Capital an asset manager or a research firm?

Quant Flow Capital is an independent macroeconomics and geopolitical research intelligence firm. We deliver uncompromised analytical clarity for capital allocators, hedge funds, family offices, and market participants globally.
