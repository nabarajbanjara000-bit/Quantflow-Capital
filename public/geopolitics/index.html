Monetary Architecture

Reserve Currency Fragmentation & De-Dollarization

The weaponization of foreign exchange reserves has prompted non-Western central banks to diversify away from G10 sovereign paper into gold, bilateral currency swaps, and local settlement mechanisms.

  • Central bank official gold reserves reached 24% of non-US allocations.
  • BRICS multi-currency trade invoicing expanding in agricultural and energy transactions.
  • US dollar's share of global payments remains dominant in swift settlements, creating liquidity divergence.

Energy Corridors

Energy Chokepoints & Inflation Pass-Through

21 million barrels of crude transit the Strait of Hormuz daily, while Red Sea disruptions re-route global container traffic around the Cape of Good Hope, structurally lifting freight rates and European energy input costs.

  • Brent crude supply elasticity constrained by spare capacity concentration in the Persian Gulf.
  • European natural gas storage dynamics vulnerable to Middle Eastern and US Gulf LNG export interruptions.
  • Direct +0.4% headline CPI push per sustained $10/bbl crude increase across G10 economies.

Fiscal & Sovereign

Fiscal Dominance & Term Premium Escalation

Persistent fiscal deficits across the United States, United Kingdom, and Europe mean sovereign debt issuance outpaces organic central bank absorption, driving long-dated bond yields higher even during rate-cutting cycles.

  • US annual net Treasury issuance exceeds $2 trillion, steepening 2s10s and 10s30s curves.
  • Bank of Japan exit from negative rates and quantitative easing reduces domestic demand for US Treasuries.
  • Debt servicing costs now rival defense spending in major Western national budgets.

Supply Chains

Critical Minerals & Technological Protectionism

The race for semiconductor autonomy, advanced AI compute infrastructure, and critical battery minerals (lithium, cobalt, copper, rare earths) has created a modern mercantilism governed by national security directives.

  • Export restriction regimes on graphite, gallium, and germanium impacting high-tech trade balances.
  • Taiwan Strait maritime corridor carries over 40% of the world’s container fleet and ~90% of advanced chips.
  • Capital expenditures in domestic semiconductor fab construction altering industrial production numbers.
